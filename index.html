Our Clinic

In Coquitlam since 2006.
Led by a physician.

At MBK Medical Skin Care we are always focused on your long-term health and satisfaction, and we advise you accordingly about all your options to enhance your natural beauty and youthful appearance. We use minimal dosages of products and treatments to achieve optimal results in a safe and responsible manner. We are dedicated to listen to you. All this happens in a warm and relaxing atmosphere of our cosmetic clinic.

Established in 2006 by Dr. Margret B-Kwiatkowski, MD, CCFP, FCFP. We use state-of-the-art equipment (from Cynosure) and the highest quality, reputable brand-name products for every procedure. Most of our patients are from Coquitlam, Burnaby, Port Moody, New Westminster, Maple Ridge and Langley — but everybody from British Columbia is welcome.
